Northern Lights Media Halloween DFX Contest

Create a Halloween-themed DFX project in the software of your choice, then share it with the Northern Lights Media community. The contest is open to all NKU students.

Submission deadline
Sunday, October 25, 2026
Showcase and awards
Thursday, October 29, 2026
6:15–9:00 PM
Location
Griffin Hall 231
The DFX Lab
Eligibility
All NKU students

The challenge

Make a Halloween-themed DFX creation using any software. You may submit up to two pieces, and each piece must have been created within the past year. Models, animation, games, renders, and other digital work are welcome.

Awards

  • Coziest CreationWarm, cozy Halloween atmosphere
  • Spookiest CreationSpooky atmosphere and impact
  • Best in ShowOverall craft, originality, and presentation

Submit your work

Submit up to two pieces per student, created within the past year. Submissions are due Sunday, October 25. Entry instructions will be shared by Northern Lights Media before the deadline.

Showcase night

Entries will be showcased and contest recipients announced at the October 29 club meeting. After the awards ceremony, stay for game night, and feel free to come in costume.
