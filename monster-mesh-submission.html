Northern Lights Media Halloween DFX Contest

Create a Halloween-themed DFX project using the software of your choice. The contest is open to all NKU students.

Who can enter
All NKU students
Theme
Halloween
Submission deadline
Sunday, October 25, 2026
Showcase and awards
Thursday, October 29, 2026

What to submit

Submit up to two original Halloween-themed DFX creations per student. Each piece must have been created within the past year. Any software is welcome; models, animation, games, renders, and other digital work are encouraged.

Awards

  • Coziest CreationWarm, cozy Halloween atmosphere
  • Spookiest CreationSpooky atmosphere and impact
  • Best in ShowOverall craft, originality, and presentation
