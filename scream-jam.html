The Challenge

Create a spooky video game in just one week! Whether you want to make a 3D horror walk, a retro 2D platformer, a text adventure, or something totally experimental, any genre and engine are welcome.

Who Can Join?

Everyone! Game development brings together 3D modelers, animators, coders, 2D artists, musicians, sound designers, and writers. Beginners and experienced creators alike are encouraged to participate.

Kickoff & Team Formation

Join us in person on Friday, October 16 at 6:00 PM in the DFX Lab (GH 231). We’ll discuss the jam, share resources, and help connect solo creators looking to form a team before work begins.

Submissions on itch.io

All projects will be submitted through our itch.io jam page by October 23rd. Jam link and theme guidelines will be provided at kickoff.
