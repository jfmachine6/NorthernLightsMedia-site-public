Northern Lights Media presents

Event Series Agenda

The Griffin Hall Haunting

DFX Halloween Festival

A two-week Halloween creative festival uniting game makers, 3D sculptors, and digital artists across Northern Kentucky University. Spooky and fun DFX events leading up to Halloween!

Kickoff: Friday, October 16, 2026

😱 Friday, 10/16 | Scream Jam 2026 🎮

Week-long game jam starting Oct. 16th. Join Northern Lights Media and the Game Developers Guild and work with your friends or by yourself to create a spooky video game in 7 days. Submissions due Oct. 23rd; hosted on itch.io.

📍 DFX Lab GH 231 ⏰ 10/16 at 6:00 PM Kickoff 🌐 Hosted on itch.io
View dedicated Scream Jam page →

Workshop: Thursday, October 22, 2026

🎃 Thursday, 10/22 | Digital Pumpkin Carving & Halloween Modeling 💀

Stop by the DFX lab for Northern Lights Media's Digital Pumpkin Carving night where we'll have a guided workshop to digitally carve a Jack'o'Lantern to be 3D printed at the Stego Studio. Also feel free to stop by and model anything Halloween related, you never know when there might be a Halloween-themed DFX contest to submit something for...

📍 DFX Lab GH 231 ⏰ 10/22 from 6:15 PM – 7:15 PM 🖨️ Stego Studio 3D Prints

Finale & Party: Thursday, October 29, 2026

🧟 Thursday, 10/29 | The Monster MESH 🪩

13 days after the start of The Griffin Hall Haunting, the bell tolls for The Monster MESH, NLM's first-ever DFX Halloween Contest. Submit Halloween-themed DFX pieces by Sunday, 10/25, and a council of GHOULISH 👻 judges may deem you worthy of an award, given out at The Monster MESH party/game night on Thursday.

📍 DFX Lab GH 231 ⏰ 10/29 from 6:15 PM – 9:00 PM 🏆 Submissions due Sun, 10/25
