The DFX Lab

The challenge

Make a Halloween-themed DFX creation. Any software is welcome, and any NKU student may enter.

Proposed awards

Three categories are being considered: Coziest Creation, Spookiest Creation, and Best in Show. Final category names and judging details need approval.

Proposed submission formats

Formats under consideration include MP4, MOV, JPG, PNG, BLEND, MA, MB, ZIP for game projects, OBJ, FBX, and PDF. Final file requirements are not set.

How to submit

Submissions are planned to open October 15 and close October 25. The entry form, deadline time, accepted formats, and file-sharing instructions are still being finalized.

Awards and showcase

Contest recipients will be announced and entries showcased during the October 29 meeting. Game night follows the awards and showcase.
